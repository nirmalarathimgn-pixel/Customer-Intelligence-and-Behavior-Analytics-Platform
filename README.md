# 👥 User Engagement & Customer Behavior Analytics Platform

## 📌 Project Overview

Understanding customer behavior is critical for improving retention, increasing revenue, and building effective marketing strategies. This project provides a comprehensive customer analytics solution that transforms transactional data into actionable business insights.

Using SQL, Python, and Power BI, this project analyzes customer purchasing patterns, loyalty behavior, geographic distribution, and customer value through advanced segmentation and RFM analysis.

The dashboard enables business stakeholders to better understand customer behavior and make data-driven decisions to maximize customer lifetime value.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Businesses often struggle to answer questions such as:

* Who are the most valuable customers?
* Which customers are likely to become inactive?
* What customer segments generate the most revenue?
* Which regions contribute the highest sales?
* How can customer retention be improved?

This project addresses these challenges through customer intelligence and behavior analytics.

* A small percentage of customers contribute a large portion of total revenue.
* Loyal customers generate significantly higher lifetime value.
* Customer purchasing behavior varies across geographic regions.
* RFM analysis effectively identifies high-value and at-risk customers.
